Child Safety Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. This applies to Ruach, made by Highnestbird.

1. Ages

Ruach is for people aged 13 and over. Selling, Business accounts, publishing and Ruach Pay are for people aged 18 and over. We use the date of birth you give us when you sign up, so please be honest.

2. Zero tolerance

Sexual content involving anyone under 18 is never allowed on Ruach. We remove it, close the account and report it to the authorities.

3. Protecting young people

Do not contact young people for sexual or exploitative reasons, ask them for private photos or information, or try to meet them in secret. Young people should never share their address, school or phone number with people they do not know.

4. Reporting

If you see something that worries you, use the Report button on the post or profile, or email highnestbird@gmail.com. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or child protection service straight away.

5. Parents and guardians

If you are a parent or guardian and you want an account removed, or you have a concern, email us and we will act quickly.
