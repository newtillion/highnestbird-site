Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. This applies to Ruach, made by Highnestbird.

1. Who we are

Highnestbird makes and runs Ruach and is responsible for your personal information in Ruach. You can reach us at highnestbird@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account details: username, email, password (stored securely by our sign-in provider), date of birth, country, name, picture, banner and bio. What you create: posts, stories, comments, messages, photos, videos, articles and listings. Activity: when you were last active, read receipts and the people you block, add or follow, unless you hide them. For Business and publisher applications: the details, ID photo and documents you send. Reports and feedback you send us. We also record that you accepted these terms, and when.

3. How we use it

To run Ruach and show your profile and content to other people according to your settings. To suggest people from your country. To review applications and reports and keep Ruach safe. To follow the law. We rely on your agreement, on our need to provide the service you asked for, and on our legitimate interest in keeping Ruach safe.

4. Who can see what

Your profile and posts can be seen by signed-in Ruach users. Your stories are shown to your friends and followers, and announcements from the Ruach team are shown to everyone. Messages are seen by the people in the chat. ID photos and business documents are seen only by the Ruach team and are deleted once we decide on your application.

5. Where it is stored and who helps us

Your data is stored with Google Firebase, which runs sign-in and our database, and with Cloudinary, which stores some photos and videos. These companies may process data in other countries. Videos embedded from YouTube are loaded from YouTube. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Ruach stores small files in your browser only to keep you signed in and to remember your settings.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the services that help us run Ruach, with the authorities when the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account exists. When you delete your account we remove your profile, username, posts, stories, listings and friend and follow links. Messages you sent may remain for the people who received them. Reports, and the records of what you accepted, may be kept for as long as needed for safety and legal reasons. Copies of photos and videos held by our media service can take time to disappear.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your information, and you can object to how we use it. You can download a copy of your data in Settings, and delete your account in Settings. For anything else, email us and we will answer within a reasonable time. If you are not happy, you can complain to your data protection authority, such as the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

9. Keeping your information safe

We use secure connections and access rules so people can only see what they should. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us if you notice anything wrong.

10. Children

Ruach is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from younger children. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will remove it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Email highnestbird@gmail.com.
