Marketplace Rules

Last updated 15 November 2026. This applies to Ruach, made by Highnestbird.

1. Who can sell

Business accounts and Content Creator accounts can list items. Sellers must be 18 or older. Personal accounts can browse and message sellers. A verified badge means we reviewed the seller’s documents. It is not a guarantee.

2. Honest listings

Use your own photos. Describe the item truthfully, including its condition, the real price and your real location. Mark items as sold when they are gone.

3. What you cannot sell

Weapons, ammunition and explosives; illegal drugs and drug equipment; stolen goods; counterfeit or fake items; fake IDs, certificates or documents; human body parts; protected wildlife and wildlife products; medicines that need a licence; adult sexual products or services; items that promote hatred or violence; personal data or accounts; and anything illegal where you or the buyer live.

4. Ruach’s role

Ruach is a place where people can meet. We are not the buyer or the seller, we do not hold money, and we do not check items. Dealings are between the buyer and the seller.

5. Stay safe

Meet in a busy public place, bring a friend, check the item before you pay, and never send money to someone you do not know. Ruach will never ask for your password, PIN or one-time codes.

6. Fees and taxes

Ruach does not charge fees for listings at the moment. Sellers are responsible for any taxes, licences or permits that apply to what they sell.

7. Reporting and removal

Report suspicious listings with the Report button. We may remove listings and close accounts that break these rules.
