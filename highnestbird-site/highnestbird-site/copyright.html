Copyright Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. This applies to Ruach, made by Highnestbird.

1. Our approach

Highnestbird respects the rights of creators and expects Ruach users to do the same. Only post photos, videos, music, writing and other material that you created or that you have permission to use.

2. Reporting copyright infringement

If you believe something on Ruach copies your work without permission, email highnestbird@gmail.com with: your name and contact details; a description of your work; a link to, or description of, where the content is on Ruach; a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not allowed by you, your agent or the law; a statement that the information you gave is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner; and your signature (typed is fine).

3. What we do

When we get a complete notice, we will promptly remove or disable the content and may tell the person who posted it. We may share your notice with them, including your contact details.

4. Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, email us with: your name and contact details; where the content was before it was removed; a statement that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or wrongly identified; and your agreement that a court in your country can hear any dispute. We may restore the content unless the person who reported it takes legal action.

5. Repeat infringers

We will close the accounts of people who repeatedly infringe other people’s rights.

6. Trademarks and fake pages

Do not use another brand’s name or logo to mislead people. Report impersonation using the Report button or by email.

7. False claims

Do not send a notice or counter-notice you know is false. You may be responsible for the harm it causes.

8. Note

This policy is a general guide and is not legal advice. Copyright rules differ between countries.
