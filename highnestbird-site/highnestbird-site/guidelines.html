Community Guidelines

Last updated 15 November 2026. This applies to Ruach, made by Highnestbird.

1. Be respectful

Treat people kindly. Do not harass, bully, threaten or shame anyone. Disagreeing is fine; attacking people is not.

2. No hate

Do not attack people because of their race, tribe, nationality, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or any other personal trait.

3. No violence or dangerous activity

Do not threaten, encourage or celebrate violence, terrorism, self-harm or dangerous challenges. If you or someone you know is in danger, contact your local emergency services.

4. Children come first

Never post or share sexual content involving anyone under 18. This is never allowed, and we report it to the authorities. Do not contact children for sexual or exploitative reasons.

5. No sexual or graphic content

Do not post pornography, sexually explicit material or extremely graphic violence.

6. No illegal activity, scams or fraud

Do not sell or promote illegal goods or services. Do not run scams, ask people for money under false pretences, or pretend to be a bank, company, official or Ruach staff.

7. No impersonation or fake accounts

Use your real identity or a clearly honest page. Do not copy someone else’s name, photo or business.

8. Respect privacy

Do not share other people’s private information, such as phone numbers, addresses, IDs or private messages, without their permission.

9. No spam or misleading content

Do not spam, flood chats, run fake giveaways, or spread false information that could cause harm.

10. Respect rights

Only post what you have the right to share. See our Copyright Policy.

11. What happens if you break the rules

Depending on how serious it is, we may remove content, limit features, suspend your account or close it for good. Serious cases may be reported to the authorities. You can appeal by emailing highnestbird@gmail.com.

12. Reporting

Use the Report button on posts, articles, listings and profiles. You can also block anyone from their chat. Reports are reviewed by the Ruach team.
